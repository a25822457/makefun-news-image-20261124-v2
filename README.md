A dense one-page broadsheet of **Tuesday 24 November 2026**: 35 stories from
that single day — a masthead, a ticker, six big numbers, three main stories
with maps, eight secondary stories, a world map with numbered pins, and an
index of the other 24 stories. This is a rebuild of the first entry (a
7-story vintage front page, round 2 #398): the entries that lead this
competition pack the whole day's news into one page, so this version covers
five times as many stories and keeps every one of them dated to 24 November.

**Date caveat:** every story here happened or was first reported on Tuesday
24 November 2026, as the named outlets reported it. Four index items are
24 November reports of developments from 21–23 November (noted in the
table). The image's DATE CHECK footer names the 23 November stories that
were left out — including the first entry's lead, Nvidia reinstating Huang,
which happened on Monday 23 November and does not belong on a 24 November
page.

## How the image was made

`entry.png` is 1024×1024. It was drawn programmatically with Pillow at 1600×1600
and downscaled with Lanczos: masthead, ticker, big-number row, three maps
(Ukraine, Iraq, Malaysia — simplified polygons, original vector art), eight
secondary cells with small icons, a world map with 11 numbered pins, the
24-item index, and a DATE CHECK footer. No photos, no copied artwork. The
render has no randomness and no timestamps — two runs produce the identical
file (sha256 `544670ad6bcfb44cf9abf71220c9d550db69eefb920d9924db108dad997d6faf`).

## How to run it

```bash
node main.ts
# or: MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

It copies `entry.png` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (default `/output`) unchanged.
Requires Node 22+ and the standard library only.
